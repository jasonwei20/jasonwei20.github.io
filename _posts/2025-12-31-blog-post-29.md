## Work
As the RL infra at Meta started to get built, I took on more tasks at Meta and I wanted to push myself to work more.
The work ethic of Chinese people at Meta is inspiring.
I can tell that most people continue working after they go home.
There is one infra engineer that is basically always in the office and working, and I would say goodnight to him whenever I went home and he would still be working!

I got into a bit of a rhythm of working 40+ hour weeks. 
I thought I had worked hard in my AI residency, which was the last time I tried to work super hard (at OpenAI, I also worked hard but there was a lot more time spent in meetings and my relationship still took some time), but I think I was able to easily pass that now. 
Probably because going to the office makes it really easy to focus and the workload was pretty big anyways.
Towards the end of each week though, I could feel my brain was a bit tired, and in order to work more hours I didn't prioritize exercise as much as I should have.

### AI race v1
I hosted an exercise event of sorted that I called "race to AGI". 
It was a larger scale follow-up to the lifting event I had last quarter with 7 people. 
Around 25 people came and I set up a few events for four teams: pull ups, tug of war, relay race, and then capture the flag.
Everyone seemed to have a lot of fun!
The lesson for me here is that you can host any event that you want!
I always like to relive being a kid again, and I think doing something unique is probably more memorable than just a regular party or brunch.
I was so nervous planning the event but now I am really motivated to host more events in the future.
